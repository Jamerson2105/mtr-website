Projects

What we're building, and what we've built.

Current project

Autonomous Drowning-Search Robot

An autonomous surface vessel that speeds up the locate-and-rescue window on open water. It uses LiDAR for obstacle avoidance and fused GNSS/IMU for waypoint navigation, built on ROS 2 and tested in simulation.

  • ROS 2
  • LiDAR
  • GNSS/IMU
  • Simulation
Past project · Form & Function 2025

Soft Medical Triaging Robot

Performs tasks to assess how urgently someone needs care, using body temperature, oxygen saturation and heart rate. Won 2nd Place and the Audience Award.

  • Soft robotic gripper
  • Pneumatic actuation
  • Snap-on vitals wristband
