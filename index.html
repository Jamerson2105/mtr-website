University of Waterloo Student Design Team

Robotics that responds when it matters most.

UW MedTechRobotics designs and builds robotic systems for public safety, emergency response and healthcare, giving students hands-on experience solving real problems.

See our projects Join the team

Who we are

We're a multidisciplinary team of Waterloo engineering, science and business students. From autonomous navigation to patient monitoring, we take projects from concept to tested prototype, combining software, electronics and mechanical design.

Projects

What we're building, and what we've built.

Current

Autonomous Boat

An autonomous surface vessel for water-based search and response. Uses LiDAR for obstacle avoidance and fused GNSS/IMU for waypoint navigation, built on ROS 2 and tested in simulation.

  • ROS 2
  • LiDAR
  • GNSS/IMU
